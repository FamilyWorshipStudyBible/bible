Zophar Rebukes Job

1Then answered Zophar the Naamathite, and said, 2Should not the multitude of words be answered? and should a man full1 of talk be justified? 3Should thy lies2 make men hold their peace? and when thou mockest, shall no man make thee ashamed? 4For thou hast said, My doctrine is pure, and I am clean in thine eyes. 5But oh that God would speak, and open his lips against thee; 6And that he would shew thee the secrets of wisdom, that they are double to that which is! Know therefore that God exacteth of thee less than thine iniquity deserveth.

7Canst thou by searching find out God? canst thou find out the Almighty unto perfection? 8It is as high3 as heaven; what canst thou do? deeper than hell; what canst thou know? 9The measure thereof is longer than the earth, and broader than the sea. 10If he cut4 off, and shut up, or gather together, then who can hinder5 him? 11For he knoweth vain men: he seeth wickedness also; will he not then consider it? 12For vain6 man would be wise, though man be born like a wild ass’s colt.

13If thou prepare thine heart, and stretch out thine hands toward him; 14If iniquity be in thine hand, put it far away, and let not wickedness dwell in thy tabernacles. 15For then shalt thou lift up thy face without spot; yea, thou shalt be stedfast, and shalt not fear: 16Because thou shalt forget thy misery, and remember it as waters that pass away: 17And thine age shall be clearer7 than the noonday; thou shalt shine forth, thou shalt be as the morning. 18And thou shalt be secure, because there is hope; yea, thou shalt dig about thee, and thou shalt take thy rest in safety. 19Also thou shalt lie down, and none shall make thee afraid; yea, many shall make suit8 unto thee. 20But the eyes of the wicked shall fail, and they shall9 not escape, and their hope shall be as the giving10 up of the ghost.

Notes

1 11:2 full…: Heb. of lips

2 11:3 lies: or, devices

3 11:8 as high…: Heb. the heights of heaven

4 11:10 cut…: or, make a change

5 11:10 hinder…: Heb. turn him away?

6 11:12 vain: Heb. empty

7 11:17 be clearer…: Heb. arise above

8 11:19 make suit…: Heb. intreat thy face

9 11:20 they shall…: Heb. flight shall perish from them

10 11:20 the giving…: or, a puff of breath

Questions for discussion

  1. What does Zophar's comparison between God's wisdom and the physical universe (heaven, Sheol, earth) symbolize?
  2. How does Zophar's promise of hope reflect the Christian concept of redemption?
  3. How does this chapter challenge your understanding of the relationship between sin, punishment, and repentance?
