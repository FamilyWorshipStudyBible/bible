The Triumphant Messiah

(Acts 4:23–31)

1Why do the heathen rage1, and the people imagine2 a vain thing?

2The kings of the earth set themselves, and the rulers take counsel together, against the LORD, and against his anointed, saying,

3Let us break their bands asunder, and cast away their cords from us.

4He that sitteth in the heavens shall laugh: the Lord shall have them in derision.

5Then shall he speak unto them in his wrath, and vex3 them in his sore displeasure.

6Yet have I set4 my king upon5 my holy hill of Zion.

7I will declare the decree6: the LORD hath said unto me, Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee.

8Ask of me, and I shall give thee the heathen for thine inheritance, and the uttermost parts of the earth for thy possession.

9Thou shalt break them with a rod of iron; thou shalt dash them in pieces like a potter’s vessel.

10Be wise now therefore, O ye kings: be instructed, ye judges of the earth.

11Serve the LORD with fear, and rejoice with trembling.

12Kiss the Son, lest he be angry, and ye perish from the way, when his wrath is kindled but a little. Blessed are all they that put their trust in him.

Notes

1 2:1 rage: or, tumultuously assemble

2 2:1 imagine: Heb. meditate

3 2:5 vex: or, trouble

4 2:6 set: Heb. anointed

5 2:6 upon…: Heb. upon Zion, the hill of my holiness

6 2:7 the decree: or, for a decree

Catechism questions on this chapter

Read the chapter, then ask the questions. Choose your catechism.

A Catechism for Boys and Girls2 questions

  1. Q. 38

    What is the covenant of grace?

    2:7–8

    The agreement God the Father made with Christ concerning His elect people, to save them from their sins.

  2. Q. 60

    Why is Christ a king?

    2:6–9

    Because He rules over us and defends us.

The Baptist Catechism1 question

  1. Q. 26

    What offices doth Christ execute as our Redeemer?

    2:6, 2:8–11

    Christ as our Redeemer executeth the offices of a prophet, of a priest, and of a king, both in his estate of humiliation and exaltation.

An Orthodox Catechism4 questions

  1. Q. 20

    Is then salvation restored by Christ to all men who perished in Adam?

    2:12

    Not to all: but to those only who by a true faith are ingrafted into him, and receive his benefits.

  2. Q. 30

    Why is he called Christ, that is, anointed?

    2:6

    Because he was ordained of the Father, and anointed of the holy Ghost the chief prophet and teacher, who hath opened unto us the secret counsel, & all the will of his Father concerning our redemption, and the high priest, who with that one only sacrifice of his body hath redeemed us, and doth continually make intercession to his Father for us; and a king, who ruleth us by his Word and Spirit, and defendeth and maintaineth that salvation which he hath purchased for us.

  3. Q. 50

    What profit is this glory of our head Christ unto us?

    2:9

    First, that through his Holy Spirit he poureth upon us his members heavenly graces, then that he shieldeth and defendeth us by his power against all our enemies.

  4. Q. 139

    What is required unto that prayer which shall please God, and be heard of him?

    2:11

    That we ask of the only true God, who hath manifested himself in his Word, all things which he hath commanded to be asked of him with a true affection and desire of our heart, and through an inward feeling of our need and misery cast ourselves down prostrate in presence of his divine majesty, and build ourselves on this sure foundation, that we though unworthy, yet for Christ’s sake are certainly heard of God, even as he hath promised us in his Word.
