The Suffering Servant

(Acts 8:26–40; 1 Peter 2:21–25)

1Who has believed our message?

And to whom has the arm of the LORD been revealed?1

2He grew up before Him like a tender shoot,

and like a root out of dry ground.

He had no stately form or majesty to attract us,

no beauty that we should desire Him.

3He was despised and rejected by men,

a man of sorrows, acquainted with grief.

Like one from whom men hide their faces,

He was despised, and we esteemed Him not.

4Surely He took up our infirmities

and carried our sorrows;2

yet we considered Him stricken,

struck down by God, and afflicted.

5But He was pierced for our transgressions,

He was crushed for our iniquities;

the punishment that brought us peace was upon Him,

and by His stripes we are healed.3

6We all like sheep have gone astray,4

each one has turned to his own way;

and the LORD has laid upon Him

the iniquity of us all.

7He was oppressed and afflicted,

yet He did not open His mouth.

He was led like a lamb to the slaughter,

and as a sheep before her shearers is silent,

so He did not open His mouth.

8By oppression and judgment He was taken away,

and who can recount His descendants?

For He was cut off from the land of the living;5

He was stricken for the transgression of My people.

A Grave Assigned

(Matthew 27:57–61; Mark 15:42–47; Luke 23:50–56; John 19:38–42)

9He was assigned a grave with the wicked,

and with a rich man in His death,

although He had done no violence,

nor was any deceit in His mouth.6

10Yet it was the LORD’s will to crush Him

and to cause Him to suffer;

and when His soul is made a guilt offering,7

He will see His offspring, He will prolong His days,

and the good pleasure of the LORD will prosper in His hand.

11After the anguish of His soul,

He will see the light of life8 and be satisfied.

By His knowledge My righteous Servant will justify many,

and He will bear their iniquities.

12Therefore I will allot Him a portion with the great,9

and He will divide the spoils with the strong,10

because He has poured out His life unto death,

and He was numbered with the transgressors.11

Yet He bore the sin of many

and made intercession for the transgressors.

Notes

1 53:1 Cited in John 12:38 and Romans 10:16

2 53:4 LXX This One bears our sins and is pained for us; cited in Matthew 8:17 and 1 Peter 2:24

3 53:5 Cited in 1 Peter 2:24

4 53:6 Cited in 1 Peter 2:25

5 53:8 LXX In humiliation He was deprived of justice. Who can recount His descendants? For His life was removed from the earth; cited in Acts 8:32–33.

6 53:9 Cited in 1 Peter 2:22

7 53:10 Or and though He makes His life a guilt offering

8 53:11 DSS (see also LXX); MT does not include the light of life.

9 53:12 Or many

10 53:12 Or numerous

11 53:12 Cited in Luke 22:37

Questions for discussion

  1. What does it mean for the Servant to be "despised and rejected"? How can we respond when we feel similarly?
  2. What does it mean for the "will of the Lord" to prosper in the Servant's hand?
  3. How does understanding the concept of atonement presented in Isaiah 53 influence your perspective on forgiveness?

Catechism questions on this chapter

Read the chapter, then ask the questions. Choose your catechism.

A Catechism for Boys and Girls2 questions

  1. Q. 46

    For whom did Christ obey and suffer?

    53:8

    For those whom the Father had given Him.

  2. Q. 48

    What kind of death did Christ die?

    the whole chapter

    The painful and shameful death of the Cross.

The Baptist Catechism1 question

  1. Q. 30

    Wherein did Christ’s humiliation consist?

    53:2–3

    Christ’s humiliation consisted in his being born, and that in a low condition; made under the law, undergoing the miseries of this life, the wrath of God, and the cursed death of the cross; in being buried, and continuing under the power of death for a time. Matt 12:40.

An Orthodox Catechism6 questions

  1. Q. 8

    Are we so corrupt that we are not at all apt to do well, and are prone to all vice?

    53:6

    Indeed we are, except we are regenerated by the holy Ghost.

  2. Q. 15

    What manner of Mediator then, and deliverer must we seek for?

    53:11

    Such a one verily as is very man, and perfectly just, and yet in power above all creatures, that is, a one who also is very God.

  3. Q. 20

    Is then salvation restored by Christ to all men who perished in Adam?

    53:11

    Not to all: but to those only who by a true faith are ingrafted into him, and receive his benefits.

  4. Q. 36

    What. believest thou when thou sayest he suffered?

    53:12

    That he all the time of his life which he lead in the earth, but especially at the end thereof sustained the wrath of God, both in and soul, against the sin of mankind, that he might by his passion, as the only propitiatory sacrifice, deliver our body and soul from everlasting damnation, and purchase us the favour of God, righteousness, and everlasting life.

  5. Q. 37

    For what cause should he suffer under Pilate, as being his judge?

    53:4–5

    That he being innocent and condemned before a civil judge, might deliver us from the severe judgment of God which remained for all men.

  6. Q. 43

    Why is there added, he descended into hell?

    53:10

    That in my greatest pains and most grievous temptations I may support myself with this comfort, that my Lord Jesus Christ hath delivered me (by the unspeakable distresses, torments and terrors of his soul, into which he was plunged both before, and then especially when he hanged on the cross) from the straits and torments of hell.
