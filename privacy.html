Privacy

The Family Worship Bible collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

The only things it remembers are your own settings: the translation you last chose, light or dark appearance, the translations ticked in the compare view, the catechism you last opened under a chapter, and the last chapter you read. These are kept on your own device and are never sent anywhere.

The app carries all of its text inside it and sends nothing anywhere. Pages you save for offline reading are kept on your device in the same way. The website is served by GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may keep ordinary server logs of requests, under its own privacy statement.
