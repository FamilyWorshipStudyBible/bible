Privacy

The Particular Baptist Study Bible collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

The only things it remembers are your own settings: the translation you last chose, light or dark appearance, the translations ticked in the compare view, and the last chapter you read. These are kept on your own device and are never sent anywhere.

The app carries all of its text inside it and does not need a connection. The website is served by GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may keep ordinary server logs of requests, under its own privacy statement.

Questions: github.com/gillbible.
