Confessions and catechisms

Three standards of the seventeenth-century Particular Baptists, in their own words, and a first catechism for children, with every scripture proof linked to the text.

  • The Second London Baptist Confession1677, adopted by the General Assembly of 1689. Thirty-two chapters.
  • A Catechism for Boys and GirlsA first catechism for children, Baptist in its teaching. Chapel Library, 1998. 134 questions.
  • The Baptist CatechismCommonly called Keach’s Catechism. 1693, in the text of the fifth edition, 1695. 114 questions.
  • An Orthodox CatechismHercules Collins, 1680: the Heidelberg Catechism revised for Baptist churches. 152 questions.
